RECOMMENDED TRAFFIC OVERHAUL FOR GTA V LEGACY 1.0.3889

Los Angeles Traffic (with DLC Cars) v2.3.1 — Legacy:
https://www.nexusmods.com/gta5/mods/1505

This is a separate third-party mod and is NOT bundled in this archive.
Download the Legacy variant, not the Enhanced variant. Follow the author's
installation instructions and back up the original game files first.

The overhaul changes popcycle.dat, popgroups.ymt and gameconfig.xml so the
Rockstar ambient population system can create denser, region-aware traffic
and select the added vehicle models.

Its full version requires OpenIV and a mods folder. The author lists Heap
Limit Adjuster, Script Hook V for DLC cars and a trainer for manually
spawning DLC cars. Packfile Limit Adjuster is also recommended where needed.
It can conflict with another custom gameconfig.xml or traffic/population mod.

Living Los Santos does not bundle or redistribute these files. Its F10 menu
only scales the ambient population configured by GTA and installed data mods.
100% means full configured density; it does not raise the engine's population
limits by itself.
